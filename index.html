EasyTonometr Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

EasyTonometr helps you record blood-pressure readings from a monitor display. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use its features.

Information Used to Recognize Readings

When you take or select a photo of a blood-pressure monitor, the app uses the image only to recognize the readings shown on the display.

To provide recognition, the selected image is sent securely to our OCR processing service hosted on Yandex Cloud. Images may contain personal information visible in the photo, so only submit images you are comfortable sharing for this purpose.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number.

Apple Health

With your permission, EasyTonometr can access blood-pressure and heart-rate data through Apple Health and save recognized or manually entered readings to the Health app. Health data is handled according to your Apple Health permissions. You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. EasyTonometr does not collect or store your payment-card details.

Your Choices

You can choose not to grant access to the camera, photo library, or Apple Health. Without camera or photo-library access, photo recognition is unavailable. Without Apple Health permission, the app cannot save readings to Apple Health. You can stop using the OCR service by choosing not to submit a photo for recognition.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The current version is available in the app and on this page.
